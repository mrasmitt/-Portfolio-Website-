UNOFLASH

A Note From the Founder

I founded UNOFLASH with one simple belief: fashion should be made for you, not just your size.

We are building a new fashion experience where customers can share their measurements and get clothing designed for their personal fit — at no additional fitting cost.

Our vision goes beyond clothing. We aim to bring unique fashion from Europe and around the world to India, while making luxury more accessible through our premium designer-wear rental collection.

We also create customized products — including T-shirts, home accessories, birthday gifts, and more — featuring your name, logo, or personal design.

Schools, coaching centres, institutes, and organizations can also contact us for custom uniforms, merchandise, accessories, and bulk requirements.
